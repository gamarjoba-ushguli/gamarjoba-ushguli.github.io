Ushguli

Where it is

At the upper edge of the village, close to St George church and Lamaria church.

Ushguli is a group of villages at the head of the Enguri valley in Svaneti, at about 2,100 metres, and its stone towers are a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Fifty metres away there is a hill, and it is the highest point of the village. From the top you see the whole of Ushguli one way and Shkhara, the highest peak in Georgia, the other.

The trail to the Shkhara glacier starts here. So does the last day of the walk from Mestia, in reverse. There is an ethnographic museum near the house, in an old Svanetian house — a machubi.
