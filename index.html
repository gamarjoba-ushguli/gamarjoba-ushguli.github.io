The table

Dinner is cooked in the house and eaten together downstairs, at a long table. You sit on wooden chairs of the kind traditionally used by the village elder, and on a bench carved by Fridon Nizharadze.

You do not have to be staying the night to eat here.

Where it is

Ushguli is a group of villages at the head of the Enguri valley in Svaneti. The house stands on the upper edge of it, next to Lamaria church. Fifty metres away is the highest point of the village, and from there you can see the whole of Ushguli in one direction and Shkhara in the other.

The path to the Shkhara glacier starts here. So does the last day of the walk from Mestia, in reverse.

要確認：Mestia からの来かた（乗合車・4WD・所要時間）を書きたいので、 お客さんに普段どう案内しているか教えてください。季節によって道が変わることも、 書き方を相談させてください。
